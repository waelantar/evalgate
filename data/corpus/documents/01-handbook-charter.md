# Northstar Operations Handbook: Charter

## Purpose
Northstar is a fictional internal service that turns verified field updates into a shared operational status board. This handbook is synthetic training material. It describes no real company, person, customer, endpoint, or production system.

## Scope
The handbook governs the Northstar control plane, its scheduled workers, the status board, and operator procedures. It does not authorize changes to unrelated services or any external system.

## Evidence rule
An operational statement is reliable only when its source section is current, its revision is named, and its scope matches the question. A plausible statement from an older revision is not current evidence.

## Source hierarchy
Current policy documents outrank historical release notes. A runbook may describe a temporary mitigation but cannot replace a current policy without an explicit revision notice.

## Service boundary
Northstar accepts signed updates from the fictional Beacon Relay, validates their schema, and publishes a summarized board. Northstar never directly controls field equipment.

## Roles
The fictional roles are coordinator, on-call operator, release steward, and auditor. Roles are procedural labels, not accounts or people, and they have only the permissions stated in this handbook.

## Review cycle
The release steward reviews current policy every quarter. A review can clarify language but must create a new document revision when it changes an operational fact.

## Non-goals
Northstar is not an emergency dispatch system, a surveillance product, a billing platform, or an autonomous agent. It cannot make safety decisions for an operator.
