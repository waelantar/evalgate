# Northstar Access Policy

## Purpose
This policy limits fictional operational access to the smallest role scope needed for an approved task. It does not describe a real identity system.

## Role grants
Coordinator, on-call operator, release steward, and auditor receive role-scoped capabilities. A person-shaped identity is intentionally absent from this corpus.

## Least privilege
Board readers may read summaries. Operators may acknowledge quarantines. Release stewards may execute approved releases. Auditors may inspect evidence references. No role receives unrestricted data export.

## Credential handling
Credentials are issued and revoked by an external fictional access process. Northstar applications must not log, embed, echo, or store credentials in handbook examples.

## Elevation
Temporary elevation requires a change or incident reference, a bounded duration, and review by a different role. Standing emergency administrator access is prohibited.

## Review
The coordinator reviews role assignments quarterly using aggregate role records. The handbook provides no roster, email address, phone number, or individual identity.

## Offboarding analogue
When a role no longer needs access, its grant is removed through the fictional access process. The service confirms removal by capability, not by collecting personal details.

## Hard negative
This handbook does not specify password rules, MFA vendors, identity-provider configuration, or retention of authentication audit logs.
