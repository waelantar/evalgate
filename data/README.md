# Governed data

No corpus, golden case, embedding, or evaluation result is included in the foundation.

- `corpus/` is owned by EG-004 after the full CC0-1.0 legal text and manifest are approved.
- `golden/` is owned by EG-009 after evidence identity and review rules are frozen.

Do not add scraped, confidential, proprietary, or personal data. A generated fixture is not automatically safe or correctly licensed; review its content and provenance before commit.
